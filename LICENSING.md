This software is offered under **two** licences. You may use it under **whichever one fits you**:

| Licence | File | Who it covers, free of charge |
|---|---|---|
| **PolyForm Small Business 1.0.0** | `LICENSE.md` | Individuals and small businesses (fewer than 100 people and under about USD 1M revenue, per the licence), for any purpose, including commercial |
| **PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0** | `LICENSE-NONCOMMERCIAL.md` | Anyone — including universities, research labs, hospitals, charities, public bodies and hobbyists — for any **noncommercial** purpose |

**Everyone else** (a larger company using it for a commercial purpose) needs a separate commercial licence from the
licensor named in the Required Notice.

**Why:** the goal is capable, verifiable AI on the computers people already own, shaped by any person or community — without
handing free R&D to the largest platforms. These licences are **source-available**, not "open source" in the OSI sense;
the use limits are deliberate anti-monopoly terms.

**What the licences do not cover:** the knowledge you compile with this software. See `DATA-AND-SHARDS.md`. The shard file
format is a separate, openly licensed specification: `SHARD-FORMAT.md` (CC BY 4.0).
